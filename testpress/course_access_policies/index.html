How course access policies work

What a policy does

A policy gives a group of staff the same permissions on a set of courses. You set it up once, instead of assigning every course to every person.

Why use it

  • With many courses, adding staff course by course takes a long time, and every new course needs it again.
  • A policy on All courses also covers courses created later, so a role like Head Staff never waits for access.
  • Staff work from their own login, so no one needs to share the admin account.

How to set it up

  1. Create a policy and name it after the role, for example Head Staff or CA Inter Faculty.
  2. Choose All courses, or pick the courses it covers.
  3. Tick what staff can do in those courses, such as edit contents or clone.
  4. Add the staff who should get this access.

Good to know

  • Viewing the course is always included.
  • A person can be in more than one policy. They get everything those policies give.
  • Changing a policy changes access for everyone in it.
  • Assigning staff from a course's Staffs page works as before.
  • Staff also need a role that can open Courses.
