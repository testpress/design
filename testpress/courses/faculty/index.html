How faculty works

Why add instructors

Students often choose a course because of who teaches it. Showing your instructors' experience and achievements helps them trust the course and decide to buy.

What students see

Before they buy a course, students see who will teach them: each instructor's photo, name, subjects, years teaching and short description. Opening an instructor shows their highlights, qualifications, about, and intro video if there is one.

How to set it up

  1. Add an instructor from your staff or mentors, and fill in their profile.
  2. On their page, assign the courses they teach.
  3. For each course, add the subject they teach in it, and an intro video if you have one.

Good to know

  • A profile is written once and shown in every course they're assigned to. Edit it in one place.
  • The subject and intro video are set for each course, so they can differ between courses.
  • Faculty is for display only. It doesn't give anyone access to a course; use Add Staffs for that.
  • Removing an instructor means students no longer see them on any course. Their staff account stays.
