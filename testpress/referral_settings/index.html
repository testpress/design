Refer & Earn

View only

Only admins with the Manage settings permission can change Refer & Earn.

Reward students who bring new students

Students invite their friends. When a friend joins and buys a product, the friend gets a discount and the student gets credits for their next purchase.

For example: friend gets ₹300 off, student gets ₹200 in credits. You choose fixed amounts or a percentage.

Students can't invite new friends. They keep their credits, and friends who already joined still get their discount.

How Refer & Earn works

  1. 1. A student invites a friend

    Students who have bought a product, or all students if you allow it.

  2. 2. The friend buys with a discount

    On their first purchase of any product.

  3. 3. The student gets credits

    For their next purchase. Cancelled if the friend's order is refunded.

Nothing to pay upfront. The discount and credits come off prices at checkout, and you choose the amounts.

Turn off Refer & Earn?

Students won't be able to invite new friends. They keep their credits, and friends who already joined still get their discount.
