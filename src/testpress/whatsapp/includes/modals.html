{# First visit: what the feature does (opened from "How it works" next to the title) #} {% call modal(id="wa-how-feature", title="How WhatsApp messages work", confirm_text="", cancel_text="Got it") %}

Testpress sends these messages to students on WhatsApp automatically:

  • Login OTP

    Students who sign in with their phone number get their login code on WhatsApp.

  • Account credentials

    When you create a student account, the username and password go to the student on WhatsApp.

  • Abandoned cart

    If a student leaves a course purchase unfinished, they get a reminder to complete it.

  • You choose which of these go out, and can turn each one off any time.
  • Delivery and charges for every message show on WhatsApp usage.
