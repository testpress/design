Reward students who bring new students

Students invite their friends. When a friend joins and buys a product, the friend gets a discount and the student gets credits for their next purchase.

{# Labelled as an example so it doesn't read as a fixed value; the admin picks the amounts while setting up #}

For example: friend gets ₹300 off, student gets ₹200 in credits. You choose the amounts.
