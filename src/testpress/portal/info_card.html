How it works

  • Portals lets you add links to other platforms and resources in the Testpress student and visitor portals.
  • Add a name and website address for each link.
  • Enable Portals to make the links available on your website.
  • Choose whether each link is available to logged-in students, visitors, or both.
  • Use SSO for supported platforms, and disable links anytime to hide them without deleting them.
