{# How it works: everything an admin might wonder, kept out of the page itself. #} {% call modal(id="about-faculty", title="How faculty works", confirm_text="Got it", show_cancel=false) %}

What students see

Before buying this course, students see who teaches it: each teacher's photo, name, subjects and a short introduction. They can open a teacher to read more and play their intro video.

Set once, used everywhere

  • For this course only: the subjects they teach here and their intro video.
  • Same in every course: their photo, short description, years teaching, highlights, qualifications and about. Change it once and every course they teach shows the update.

Who you can add

Your staff and mentors. Adding a teacher here only shows them to students; it doesn't change what they can open or edit in the course.
