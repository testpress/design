{# How it works: the display-only rule, said once and plainly. #} {% call modal(id="about-faculty", title="About faculty", confirm_text="Got it", show_cancel=false) %}

What faculty do

  • Faculty tell students who teaches a course, before they buy it.
  • They show on the product page of every product that includes the course, with their subject and intro video.
  • Someone who teaches in several courses appears once, with all their subjects.

What faculty don't change

Faculty are for display only. Adding or removing someone doesn't give or take away access to a course. To let staff edit a course, use Add Staffs.

Shared profile, per-course details

  • Photo, description, qualifications, highlights and about are shared by every course the person teaches in.
  • Subject, intro video and order are set separately in each course.
