What students see

On a course's page, before they buy, students see the instructors of that course: their photo, name, the subjects they teach in it, years teaching and short description. Opening an instructor shows their highlights, qualifications, about, and intro video if there is one.

How to set it up

  1. Add an instructor from your staff or mentors, and fill in their profile.
  2. On their page, assign the courses they teach.
  3. For each course, check the subjects they teach in it and add an intro video if you have one.

Good to know

  • A profile is written once and shown in every course they're assigned to. Edit it in one place.
  • Subjects and intro videos can be different for each course.
  • Faculty is for display only. It doesn't give anyone access to a course; use Add Staffs for that.
  • Removing an instructor takes them off every course page. Their staff account stays.
