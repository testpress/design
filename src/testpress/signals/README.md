# Testpress Signals

UI design and frontend prototypes for Testpress Signals.

## Purpose

Signals helps salespeople identify students showing purchase interest,
prioritize sales Opportunities, and take the next action toward conversion.

## V1 Role

The current V1 designs are focused on the **Salesperson** role.

## Pages

| Page | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Sales Queue | Daily prioritized sales work |
| Opportunities | View and manage Opportunities |
| Unclaimed Opportunities | Discover Opportunities available to claim |
| My Results | View conversions and incentives |
| Student Messaging | Communicate with students |
