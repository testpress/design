Send TPSentinel updates to your systems

When something changes, such as a candidate's integrity result, TPSentinel sends it to your endpoint right away. Your system no longer has to look it up.

  • Choose the events and workspaces to send
  • Every request is signed, so you can check it came from us
  • Failed requests are retried, and you see each result here
