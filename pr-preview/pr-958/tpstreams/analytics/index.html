How to read content performance

This section shows how your video content is performing over time. Views show how many times your videos were watched, while watch time shows the total time viewers spent watching. Use the patterns below to understand whether your content is attracting views and driving meaningful watch time.

Pattern Chart Status What this means What to do
Views ↑
Watch time ↑
Good Your videos are getting more views and viewers are spending more time watching them. Overall content consumption is growing. Continue creating and promoting content similar to the videos performing well.
Views ↑
Watch time ↓
Attention Your videos are getting more views, but total watch time is decreasing. More views are not translating into deeper viewing. Review which videos are driving views and check whether viewers are dropping off early.
Views ↓
Watch time ↑
Attention Your videos are getting fewer views, but the viewers who watch are spending more time with the content. Identify the videos generating strong watch time and focus on increasing their reach.
Views →
Watch time →
Attention Overall content consumption is stable. Views and watch time are not showing significant growth or decline. Review your best-performing content and look for opportunities to increase reach and engagement.
Views →
Watch time ↓
Attention Views are stable, but viewers are spending less total time watching your content. Review recent content performance and investigate whether viewers are watching for shorter periods.
Views ↓
Watch time →
Attention Your content is receiving fewer views, while total watch time remains relatively stable. Focus on increasing the reach of videos that are already generating meaningful watch time.
Views ↓
Watch time ↓
Critical Both views and total watch time are declining. Overall video consumption is decreasing. Review content performance, identify what has changed, and focus on improving both reach and viewer engagement.
