  • Use SSO for supported platforms, and disable links anytime to hide them without deleting them.

About SSO

Single Sign-On with Testpress

What is SSO?

SSO lets students who are signed in to Testpress access a compatible platform without signing in again.

When should I use it?

Use SSO when the destination supports Testpress SSO and its technical team has configured the integration.

How it works

  1. 1 Student signs in to Testpress.
  2. 2 Student opens the Portal link.
  3. 3 Testpress sends a signed, time-limited sign-in request.
  4. 4 The destination verifies the request and signs in the student.
Before you configure SSO

Ask the destination's technical team to confirm that it supports Testpress SSO and provide the required shared secret.

For public links or destinations with their own login, leave the SSO Secret blank.
